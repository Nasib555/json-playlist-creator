From library to lineup

Build your library

Upload batches of videos with resumable uploads, or add videos by link. Organize them into movies and series.

Schedule live channels

Play videos back to back on a shared schedule or relay a stream you’re authorized to distribute.

Publish a guide and catch-up

Generate a guide from your schedule, edit it by hand, or import XMLTV. Offer up to 30 days of catch-up.

Connect your players

Assign accounts with connection limits and expiry dates. Share via the Open JSON Playlist API, M3U or XMLTV.

Start with the ZIP

Requires PHP 8.0+ with JSON and mbstring. FFmpeg is optional for continuous live playback from your files, automatic video lengths and posters.

Download ZIP

On your computer

Extract the ZIP, open a terminal in the extracted project folder and run:

php -S 127.0.0.1:8080 router.php

Open http://127.0.0.1:8080/admin/ to complete setup.

On a Linux server

Use the included Apache rules or the sample nginx configuration. Follow the guide to set permissions and complete setup before exposing the site.
