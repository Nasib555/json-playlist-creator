## 1.0.0

First release.

- Library with chunked, resumable uploads (many files at once), links, automatic lengths and posters (ffmpeg).
- Live channels from your own videos (back to back, looping or once, continuous HLS) or from a stream link.
- Timeline preview, programme guide (automatic or by hand, copy a day, XMLTV import) and catch-up up to 30 days.
- Movies, series, categories, multiple accounts with connection limits and expiry dates.
- Open JSON Playlist API, M3U export, XMLTV guide.
- Setup wizard with terms acceptance, command-line setup, password reset and live preparation tools.
- Backups before every save, conflict-safe saving, export and import.
- Runs on Apache (.htaccess), nginx (docs/nginx.conf) or PHP's built-in server (router.php).
